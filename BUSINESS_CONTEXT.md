# Business Context - BESOUL Suite

BESOUL Suite is the internal operating system for BESOUL Fitness.

The app is not just a calendar. It is intended to integrate:

- Lead capture and CRM.
- Trial sessions.
- Client management.
- Trainer agenda.
- Groups.
- Customer booking requests.
- Trainer availability.
- Financial settlement.
- Company expenses.
- Center profitability.
- Executive dashboard.
- PWA mobile access.

## Centers

Current centers:

- Alfa Prime.
- Inacua.
- Fantasy.
- Lagunillas.

## Business goals

BESOUL Suite must help the business to:

- Reduce WhatsApp-based manual scheduling.
- Avoid unproductive gaps.
- Improve retention for clients with variable schedules.
- Make trainer availability visible in a controlled way.
- Measure revenue by trainer and center.
- Measure occupancy by center, trainer, hour and day.
- Control trainer settlements and company margins.
- Track CRM conversion.
- Professionalize the customer and trainer experience.

## Product principle

Keep the internal app stable and controlled. Add customer-facing capabilities gradually and securely.

The customer-facing reservation module is request-only at this stage. It must not allow uncontrolled direct booking until the process is validated.
